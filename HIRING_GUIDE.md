# Hiring Guide

Use this guide to hire people who can help Ayce Sushi Finder give diners a
useful, welcoming experience.

## What to look for

Prioritize candidates who demonstrate:

- A customer-first mindset and clear, friendly communication.
- Careful attention to detail when handling restaurant information, menus, and
  dietary preferences.
- Reliability: they follow through, keep information current, and ask for help
  when something is unclear.
- Respect for guests, teammates, and the diverse food traditions represented by
  sushi.
- The role-specific skills needed for the position, such as hospitality,
  restaurant operations, content maintenance, or software development.

## Recommended hiring process

1. Define the role, expected outcomes, schedule, and must-have skills before
   publishing the opening.
2. Use a consistent job description and evaluate every applicant against the
   same written criteria.
3. Screen for relevant experience and examples of dependable, considerate
   work.
4. Hold structured interviews with the same core questions for each candidate.
   Ask for specific examples rather than hypothetical claims.
5. Include a small, role-relevant work sample when appropriate. Keep it brief,
   paid when it produces usable work, and accessible to all candidates.
6. Compare candidates using a simple scorecard, check references where allowed,
   and select the person who best meets the documented needs of the role.

## Interview questions

- Tell us about a time you improved a customer or guest experience.
- How do you verify that information you publish or share is accurate?
- Describe a situation where priorities changed quickly. What did you do?
- How do you work with someone whose background or perspective differs from
  yours?
- What support would help you succeed in this role during your first month?

## Fair and respectful hiring

Base decisions on the candidate's demonstrated skills, experience, and ability
to perform the role. Avoid questions or criteria related to protected personal
characteristics. Provide reasonable accommodations throughout the process and
document the rationale for the final decision.

## Onboarding

Give each new hire a clear first-week plan, an introduction to the team, access
to the tools they need, and a named person to answer questions. Review goals
after the first month so expectations and support remain aligned.
